Penny Rounding · Legacy EZDine

Penny Rounding

As the United States transitions away from pennies, the new Penny Rounding feature for the legacy EZDine platform automatically rounds cash totals to streamline front-of-house operations. It is optional: the merchant enables it in Admin > Settings > Payments and chooses a rounding mode.

Rounding modes:

Swedish RoundingAlways Round DownAlways Round Up

Swedish Rounding is the most common standard. Always Round Down is customer-friendly. Always Round Up is pro-merchant.

✓Visual display only. If a bill is $10.02 and rounds to $10.00, the system still records $10.02. End-of-day reports, card processing and taxes stay unchanged.
✓Optional and merchant-controlled. Enable it in Admin > Settings > Payments and pick the rounding rule that fits your local requirements.
✓Clear on the FOH payment screen. A Rounded value appears next to the exact Balance and updates dynamically.
✓Clear on checks and receipts. Pre-payment checks show a Rounded Cash Total line. Final receipts show Change Due After Rounding.
✓Three rounding modes. Swedish Rounding (most common), Always Round Down (customer-friendly), or Always Round Up (pro-merchant).
